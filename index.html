Privacy Policy

CV Analyzer Pro

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CV Analyzer Pro handles information when you use our Android application. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection and storage of personal information.

1. Information We Collect

CV Analyzer Pro does not require users to create an account or provide personal registration information.

Users may select and import CV files in PDF or Word formats for analysis. The application extracts and processes the document text locally on the device in the reviewed version.

CV Analyzer Pro does not provide a CV upload feature to a developer-controlled server in the reviewed version.

2. How We Use Information

The information contained in imported CV documents is used to perform resume analysis, evaluate ATS-related criteria, and provide feedback and improvement suggestions.

The reviewed version uses predefined analysis rules and keywords to evaluate resumes.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Application preferences and usage-related settings may be stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.

CV documents and analysis reports are processed temporarily and are not designed to be permanently stored by the application in the reviewed version.

Uninstalling the application generally removes its locally stored application data.

4. Third-Party Services

The reviewed version does not show an active integration with Google AdMob or Firebase Analytics.

Although certain Firebase-related software dependencies may be included in the application, their presence alone does not mean that personal information is being transmitted to those services.

This policy will be updated if third-party services are activated in future versions.

5. Artificial Intelligence

The reviewed version does not show an active cloud-based AI service processing uploaded CV documents. Resume analysis is based on predefined rules and keywords.

If AI-powered cloud processing is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data processing and third-party services.

6. Data Security

We aim to minimize personal data collection and avoid unnecessary storage of sensitive resume information. However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

CV Analyzer Pro is a general-purpose employment and career application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

8. User Rights

Users can stop using the application and uninstall it at any time. Locally stored application preferences can generally be removed by clearing application data or uninstalling the application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page.
